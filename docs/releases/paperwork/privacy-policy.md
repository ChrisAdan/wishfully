# Wishfully privacy policy

- **Status:** Draft for v1. Hosting location and contact address are set by the maintainer.
- **Effective date:** set at publication

## Summary

The developer of Wishfully collects zero personal data. Wish lists live on the device and in the user's iCloud account.

## Where data is stored

- **On the device.** Lists, items, notes, prices and settings are stored in the app's container on the iPhone.
- **In the user's iCloud account.** When iCloud is on, Apple's CloudKit syncs the data to the user's private iCloud database. Apple stores it under the user's Apple Account and its protection settings, including Advanced Data Protection when enabled. The developer has zero access to it.
- **Shared lists.** A list owner can share a list through iCloud sharing. Participants see the shared list. Purchase marks and price notes a shopper records stay in that shopper's private iCloud database, out of the list owner's view.

## Web pages

When the user shares a web page to Wishfully, the app downloads that page over HTTPS to read the title, price and store. The request goes from the device to that website directly. Page scripts remain inert, and the page content stays on the device.

## Diagnostics

The app records diagnostic events on the device: error codes, counts, timings and crash reports provided by iOS. List content is excluded from diagnostic records. Diagnostics leave the device only when the user exports them through the Share sheet.

## Analytics and tracking

Wishfully contains Apple frameworks and its own code exclusively. It contains zero analytics, advertising or tracking code.

## Deleting data

Deleting an item moves it to the trash for 30 days, then removes it. Deleting the app removes data on the device. Data in iCloud is removed through iOS Settings, then the user's name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, then Wishfully.
